## Data and recipients

Describe the account/nickname/password-hash, device/session, pet PNG/design, message/delivery and gathering data actually processed. Explain what authenticated peers can see, who administers the server, and whether any processors or additional recipients are involved. Explain that server messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

## Retention and deletion

State the actual message lifetime, retention and hourly cleanup interval. Document account/PNG deletion, unused-file cleanup, operational logs, backup retention, restore procedures, and how previously deleted records are handled after restoration. Specify how users can exercise access/correction/deletion rights and when requests are completed.

## Security and external services

Describe TLS, allowed access ranges, administrator access, data-directory permissions, backup protection and incident handling. Personal connectors run on users' PCs rather than on this server; disclose any additional integration/hosting/transfer that you separately arrange. Do not state that all data remains in the LAN when users enable external providers or OS notifications.

## Applicable disclosures

Include locally required controller/responsible-person details, rights and complaint channels, third-party provision, processing contractors, international transfers, and material changes where applicable. Do not invent a consent requirement or claim that self-hosting/free distribution exempts the operator from applicable law.
